FAQ

Questions goblins get asked

Do I need anything besides the bot?

Yes, the free BlueStacks emulator with Clash of Clans installed. The setup wizard walks you through it and auto-detects everything.

Can my account get banned?

Automation is against Supercell's Terms of Service, so the risk is never zero. Goblins Farm only reads the screen and taps like a human, with no memory editing and no modified clients, but you use it at your own risk.

Does my PC have to stay on?

Yes. The bot runs on your machine and drives the emulator there. Many players run it overnight, the phone notifications tell you how the farming went.

Which license do I need?

The Main Base license farms your main village; the Builder Base license farms the night village (both battle stages, auto-claims the elixir cart). Each is €60, or get both for €100 with the bundle. One key covers whatever you bought, you can start with one base and buy the other later. Keys bought before the split cover the main base.

Can I move my license to a new PC?

Yes. Deactivate on the old machine (or contact us if it's dead) and activate on the new one. One device at a time.

Something doesn't work on my setup

Email us. Most issues are emulator settings and take minutes to fix. If we can't get it running on your machine, we'll make it right.
