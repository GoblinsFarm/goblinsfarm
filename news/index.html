Clash of Clans News

Updated: 26 August 2026

Update coverage with the mechanics explained. When Supercell changes a number, the interesting question is never what the number is, it is which strategies just stopped working and which just became viable. That is what this section is for.

What gets covered here

  • Balance changes, with an argument about what they actually do to the meta rather than a copy of the patch notes.
  • New content, troops, defences and Town Hall levels, and how they change attacking and base building.
  • Wiki corrections, because when the game changes, the reference pages change with it, and it is worth saying so publicly.

What does not get covered: leaks, rumours and speculation presented as fact. If something is unconfirmed it will say so, and if we get something wrong the correction stays on the page rather than quietly disappearing.
