Clash of Clans Bot, The Complete 2026 Guide

Updated: 24 August 2026

A Clash of Clans bot is a program that plays the grindy part of the game for you, finding matches, deploying troops, collecting loot, so your storages fill while you sleep. This guide covers how modern bots actually work, what happened to the old free bots, the real ban risk, and what to look for if you want one that still works in 2026.

How modern CoC bots work

Every serious bot today runs the game inside an Android emulator (usually BlueStacks) on a PC or Mac and controls it from the outside:

  1. Screen reading. The bot takes screenshots of the emulator and uses computer vision, template matching and OCR, to recognize buttons, loot amounts, and game state.
  2. Simulated input. It taps and swipes through the emulator's input channel, the same way your finger would.
  3. A decision loop. Attack, skip low-loot bases, collect, retrain, repeat, until a target you set is reached.

What good bots deliberately don't do: modify the game client, inject code, or talk to Supercell's servers directly. Those approaches are trivially detectable and get accounts banned fast. Screen-reading bots look like a very patient human.

What happened to MyBot and the free bots?

For years the default answer was MyBot.run, free, open source, Windows-only. Emulator updates and game UI changes gradually broke it, and community maintenance stalled. That's the trade-off with free bots: nobody is on the hook when this week's game update moves a button. Paid tools live and die by shipping fixes quickly. See our full MyBot alternative comparison.

Is botting safe? The honest answer

Automation violates Supercell's Terms of Service. Full stop. Any bot seller who tells you there is zero risk is lying to you. What you can do is minimize risk: use a screen-reading bot (no client modification), keep sessions human-plausible, and ideally farm on an account you could stand to lose.
