The four kinds of CoC bot in 2026

1. MyBot.run, the free classic

MyBot.run was the standard answer for years, and it earned that. It is free, open source, and genuinely full-featured: attack strategies, loot filtering, donation handling. If you are on Windows, enjoy tinkering, and do not mind downtime, it is still worth knowing about.

The problem is maintenance. MyBot is community-maintained, and that community activity has stalled. Every Clash of Clans update that moves a button or changes a screen can break a vision-based bot, and with MyBot there is no one on the hook to ship a fix. In practice that means it is frequently broken after game updates, sometimes for long stretches. It is also Windows-only, so Mac users are out entirely. We wrote a longer piece on this in our MyBot alternative guide.

2. Auto clicker apps

Generic auto clickers, on mobile or in an emulator, replay taps at fixed positions on a timer. They have no idea what is on screen. They cannot tell a village from a defeat screen, cannot skip a dead base, and cannot recover when a popup appears. Setting one up to do anything useful in Clash of Clans takes hours of fiddling, and the result still falls apart the moment anything unexpected happens. They are free or cheap, but they are not really game bots, they are macros with no game logic. High effort, low reward.

3. Mod clients and private servers

Do not touch these. Modified game clients alter the actual game software, which is about the most detectable thing you can do and puts your account at very high ban risk. Private servers are not even the real game: your progress there means nothing on your actual account, and the download sources for both are a common home for malware. Whatever these promise, the trade is bad.

Our verdict

The honest way to pick a bot in 2026 is not feature lists, it is two questions: who fixes it when the game updates, and does it run on your machine?

A vision-based bot is only as good as its last update. MyBot's features are real, but features you cannot run this week are worth nothing, and its maintenance situation means broken weeks are the norm, not the exception. Auto clickers never worked well for this game. Mod clients fail the basic risk test.

That leaves maintained, screen-reading tools. Goblins Farm is the one we can vouch for, because we build it: it gets updates when the game changes, it is the rare option that runs on macOS as well as Windows, and it automates the Builder Base, which almost nothing else does. If you are on Windows, enjoy tinkering, and your time is free, MyBot when it works costs nothing and that is a fair choice too. If you want the loot without the hobby project, a maintained paid tool is the better deal.
