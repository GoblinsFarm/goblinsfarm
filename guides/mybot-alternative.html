MyBot Alternative, What to Use in 2026

Updated: 24 August 2026

If you're searching for a MyBot alternative, you probably already know the story: the beloved free bot that farmed billions of loot for the community has been struggling to keep up with game and emulator updates. This page is an honest look at where MyBot.run stands today, and how Goblins Farm compares, including the parts where MyBot still wins.

MyBot deserves respect

Let's start there. MyBot.run was, and in some ways still is, a remarkable project. Free, open source, built and maintained by volunteers for years, with a forum community that helped thousands of players automate their farming. It set the standard for what a screen-reading Clash of Clans bot should be: no client modification, no memory editing, just computer vision and simulated taps. If you used it in its prime, you know how good it was.

Nothing here is meant to trash MyBot. The problem isn't the idea or the code quality. The problem is time.

What went wrong

A vision-based bot is in a permanent race with the game. Every Clash of Clans update that moves a button, changes a font, or redesigns a screen can break detection. Every emulator update can change how the bot captures the screen or sends input. Someone has to notice, fix it, test it, and ship it, every single time.

For a volunteer project, that's brutal. MyBot's maintenance has slowed dramatically, and the gap between "game update breaks the bot" and "community patch lands" has stretched from days to weeks to, sometimes, never. Threads asking "is MyBot working right now?" have become a genre of their own. A bot you have to babysit and hand-patch isn't really automating anything.

That's the honest trade-off of free: nobody is on the hook. Nobody owes you a fix. When the volunteers move on with their lives, as volunteers eventually do, the bot quietly dies.
