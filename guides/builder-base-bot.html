Why no other bot does this

The Builder Base is mechanically different from the main village, and every difference makes it harder to automate. Versus battles are timed and have two stages. The Battle Machine (and later heroes) must be deployed and their abilities triggered mid-fight. Defensive loot doesn't land in your storages, it accumulates in a cart you have to claim. And the whole thing lives behind a boat ride that the bot has to navigate reliably. Bots built around "find a base, drop troops, collect loot" simply don't have the machinery for any of that, so they skip the mode. That's why "builder base bot" has been a dead-end search for years.

The night-village economy, briefly

To see what a bot has to automate, it helps to understand how Builder Base loot actually flows:

  • Versus battles are the engine. You attack, your opponent's replay attacks you, and rewards flow from wins. Unlike main-village farming, there's no "nexting", you can't skip an opponent to look for a juicier base. Every match is the match you get, which is exactly why attacking every match is the optimal strategy anyway.
  • Two battle stages. Modern Builder Base fights happen across two stages. An attack that only handles stage one leaves a large share of the available percentage, and loot, on the table.
  • Defense rewards go into the elixir cart. When your base defends, the rewards accumulate in the elixir cart, which caps at 1.6M. If nobody claims it, further defensive loot overflows and is simply lost. An AFK player bleeds resources here without ever noticing.
  • Storages cap at 6.5M. Gold and elixir storages top out at 6.5M each. Farming past full storages is wasted effort.

Put together: the Builder Base rewards someone who attacks every match, finishes both stages, empties the cart regularly, and knows when to stop. That is a checklist, and checklists are what bots are for.

How Goblins Farm automates each piece

Boating over and setting up the view

The bot taps the boat to reach the night village, then auto-zooms the camera out to a known, consistent view. That last step matters more than it sounds: Goblins Farm is a screen-reading bot, computer vision (template matching and OCR) reads the emulator screen and simulated taps do the rest, so a predictable camera position is what keeps detection reliable across hundreds of battles.

Attacking every versus match

Since versus battles can't be skipped, the bot doesn't try to be clever about opponent selection, it attacks every match it's given. That's not a compromise; it's the correct play. More matches means more win rewards and more trophies-adjacent progress, and the bot never gets bored or distracted at match forty.

Both stages, hero first, abilities triggered

This is the part that kept other bots out. In each battle, Goblins Farm deploys the hero first, follows with troops, and then re-taps the deployed cards to trigger abilities at the right moment. When stage one ends, it detects the stage-two transition and runs the same deployment logic again on the second stage. Fighting both stages consistently is the difference between mediocre and solid versus results, and it's the thing a human doing chores between attacks tends to fumble.

Claiming the elixir cart

During each session the bot auto-claims the elixir cart, so defensive rewards never sit at the 1.6M cap overflowing. This is quietly one of the best reasons to automate the Builder Base at all: defensive loot is income you earn while doing nothing, but only if someone actually collects it. The bot makes "someone" permanent.

Knowing when to stop

You set gold and elixir targets, and the bot stops when it reaches them, or when your storages hit their 6.5M caps, whichever comes first. No pointless attacking with nowhere to put the loot, no session running forever. If you have phone notifications set up, you get a push when the session ends, with the loot summary.
