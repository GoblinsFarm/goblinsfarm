How the math works

No magic: attacks needed is the upgrade cost divided by your average loot per attack, rounded up. Hours is attacks divided by attacks per hour. Nights assumes an 8 hour overnight farming session. The output is only as honest as your input, so track a few real sessions and use your true average rather than a hopeful one. Loot per attack swings a lot with league and dead-base availability, which is exactly why we do not preload a number for you. What the numbers usually reveal

Run a late-game upgrade through the calculator and the result is rarely comforting. Big Town Hall upgrades routinely work out to dozens of attacks, spread over many hours, and that is for a single upgrade out of the dozens on your to-do list. Multiply by every building, every level, and every hero, and you can see why so many players stall out in the late game. The loot is gettable, the hours are the problem.
