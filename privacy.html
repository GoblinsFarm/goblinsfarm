Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

What we collect

  • When you buy: your email address and payment details. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.
  • When you activate: your license key and an anonymous device fingerprint (a one-way hash derived from your computer's name and network hardware) used solely to enforce the one-device license limit, plus activation timestamps.
  • Nothing else. The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ad trackers. The app does not collect gameplay data, screenshots, or personal files.

Phone notifications (optional)

If you enable phone notifications, the app publishes status messages to a randomly generated, unguessable topic on the ntfy.sh public service. Messages contain farming statistics only. You can disable this at any time in Settings.

Who processes data for us

  • Stripe — payment processing and receipts
  • Cloudflare — license server and database (EU jurisdiction applies)
  • Resend — sending your license key email
  • GitHub Pages — hosting this website

Retention

License records (email, key, device hash) are kept for as long as the license exists, since they are required to provide the lifetime service. Payment records are retained by Stripe per their legal obligations.

Your rights (GDPR)

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, subject to our need to keep minimal records for active licenses and legal obligations. Contact support@goblinsfarm.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

Contact

Data controller: Goblins Farm — support@goblinsfarm.com
