Longer pieces about playing Clash of Clans well. Where the wiki answers what something is and the tutorials answer how to do it, this is where we argue about what is worth doing — usually with the game's own numbers behind the argument.

What gets written here

  • Strategy, meaning which army to learn at your Town Hall and why, rather than a list of every army that exists.
  • Progression and decisions — what to upgrade first, what a Town Hall really costs, whether a purchase is worth making.
  • Numbers, summed from the game files rather than repeated from other sites. When we claim a total, the method is in the post.

What does not get written here: leaks presented as fact, and anything that needs you to already be good at the game to follow it.

Where to start

New to the game, or coming back after years away, start with starting Clash of Clans in 2026. Stuck rather than new, read why your account stalls. Curious how deep the well goes, we added up every upgrade in the game.

Is this official Supercell content?

No. This is independent fan writing and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or approved by Supercell. For official announcements and support, use Supercell's own channels.

Where do the numbers in these posts come from?

They are summed from Clash of Clans' own game files, the same source the wiki stat tables are built from. Each post states its method, and the game version is printed under every table on the site.
