Sneezy

Pet

Updated: 26 August 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

Her nose is blocked and loaded! Sneezy hangs behind her Hero due to allergies and sneezes tanky air Boogers that target buildings. She goes on the attack when her Hero falls!
