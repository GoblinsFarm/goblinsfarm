Frosty

Pets

Pet

Updated: 16 September 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

Frosty is the coolest Pet... literally. Not only does his icy breath cause enemies to chill out, but he also releases swarms of Frostmites to further slow down defenses and help his Hero.

What the numbers say

Frosty is a hero pet. It is unlocked at Pet House 5 and upgradeable through 15 levels. It takes 20 housing space, targets ground and air and reaches 3.5 tiles.

Across those levels it gains hitpoints from 2,350 to 3,800 and damage from 94 to 150.

Taking one from level 1 to 15 costs 2,455,000 Dark Elixir in total, and 77 days of build time.
