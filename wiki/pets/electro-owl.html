Electro Owl

Pets

Pet

Updated: 16 September 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

Electro Owl zaps enemy defenses into dust from great distance. He's mostly made of feathers though, so better keep him well protected!

What the numbers say

Electro Owl is a hero pet. It is unlocked at Pet House 2 and upgradeable through 15 levels. It takes 20 housing space, targets ground and air and reaches 5.5 tiles.

Across those levels it gains hitpoints from 1,600 to 3,000 and damage from 100 to 170.

Taking one from level 1 to 15 costs 1,785,000 Dark Elixir in total, and 77 days of build time.
