Air Bomb

Traps

Trap

Updated: 18 September 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

Latest invention in the field of flying pest control. This trap can blast multiple air units in a small area.

What the numbers say

Air Bomb is a trap. It is unlocked at Town Hall 5 and upgradeable through 13 levels. It occupies 1x1 tiles, triggers on air only and within 4 tiles.

Across those levels it gains its effect from 100 damage over 3 tiles to 425 damage over 3 tiles.

Taking one from level 1 to 13 costs 38,449,000 Gold in total, and 18 days 19 hours of build time.
