Lavaloon Puppet

Equipment

EpicGrand Warden

Updated: 16 September 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

Summons spooky Lavaloons

What the numbers say

Lavaloon Puppet is epic equipment for the Grand Warden. It is unlocked at Blacksmith level 1 and upgradeable through 27 levels.

Across those levels it gains hitpoints from 50 to 150 and damage from 10 to 50.

Taking one from level 1 to 27 costs 56,060 Shiny Ore, 3,720 Glowy Ore and 480 Starry Ore in total.
