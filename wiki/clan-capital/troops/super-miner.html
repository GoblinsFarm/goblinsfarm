Super Miner (Clan Capital)

Capital Troops

Clan Capital

Updated: 18 September 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

Armed with a powerful drill and a magnificent moustache, Super Miner is ready to undermine any rival Village!

What the numbers say

Super Miner is a Clan Capital troop. It is levelled by upgrading its district Barracks and upgradeable through 4 levels. It takes 25 housing space, targets ground only and attacks at melee range.

Across those levels it gains hitpoints from 3,300 to 3,900 and damage from 160 to 190.
