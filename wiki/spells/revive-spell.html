Revive Spell — Clash of Clans

Elixir

Updated: 26 August 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

Revive returns a fallen unit to the battlefield, which changes the arithmetic of committing an expensive hero or unit early. Knowing you have a second chance makes aggressive openings viable.

What it does

The spell restores a lost unit to the attack. Because the units worth reviving are the expensive ones, its practical effect is that a hero or heavy unit can be committed to a risky opening rather than held back.

How it is used

  • After committing a hero to funnel work that was always likely to kill them.
  • As insurance on an aggressive opening, which lets you take a line you would otherwise avoid.
  • Not as a habit. Spell slots are scarce, and a revive that saves a unit which was not going to matter is a wasted slot.

Frequently asked

What does the Revive Spell do?

It returns a fallen unit to the battlefield, which mainly matters for heroes and expensive units. Its real effect is strategic: knowing a second chance exists makes aggressive openings worth taking.

Is the Revive Spell worth a slot?

When your attack plan deliberately risks a hero early, yes. As a general safety net it is poor value, because spell slots are scarce and reviving a unit that was not going to change the outcome achieves nothing.
