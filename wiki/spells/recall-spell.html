Recall Spell — Clash of Clans

Elixir

Updated: 26 August 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

Recall returns troops inside its radius to your hand, letting you redeploy them elsewhere. It is the only spell that fixes a mistake after you have made it, which is a rarer property than it sounds.

Levels
7

Role
Elixir spell, utility

What it does

Troops in the circle are returned and can be deployed again. That turns a group walking the wrong way, or one stranded on the far side of a base, back into usable army.

How it is used

  • Rescuing a wandering group after the defences are dead and your troops have reverted to nearest-target behaviour, which is described in troop targeting.
  • Repositioning a Queen walk once it has done its funnel work, so the hero joins the main push instead of continuing around the edge.
  • Recovering from a bad deployment, which is the use most players discover first and the least strategic one.

Frequently asked

What does the Recall Spell do?

It returns troops inside its radius to your hand so you can deploy them again elsewhere. It is the only spell that lets you correct a deployment after the fact.

When is Recall most useful?

Usually to rescue troops that have started wandering after all defences are destroyed, and to reposition a hero or group that has finished its funnelling job so it can rejoin the main push rather than walking around the perimeter.
