Ice Block Spell — Clash of Clans

Dark Elixir

Updated: 26 August 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

The Ice Block Spell takes a single spell housing space and affects a five tile radius across six levels. That combination, very cheap in housing and a modest area, marks it as a surgical spell rather than a push-wide one.

What the numbers say

One housing space is the important figure. Spells that cost a single slot are carried for one specific job, the way Poison is carried for the defending Clan Castle, because including one costs you almost nothing from the rest of the loadout.

A five tile radius covers a compartment or a clump, not a whole push. Read together, this is a spell you drop on one problem at one moment.

Frequently asked

How much housing space does the Ice Block Spell use?

One spell housing space, with a five tile radius. That makes it cheap to include alongside larger spells such as Rage or Healing rather than competing with them for room.
