Rage Spell — Clash of Clans

Elixir

Updated: 26 August 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

Rage increases the damage and movement speed of your troops inside its radius. It is the spell that converts a push which is nearly working into one that finishes, and it is wasted more often than any other spell in the game through simple mistiming.

What it does

Troops inside the circle deal more damage and move faster for the duration. The effect applies to whatever is inside at the time, so troops walking out of the circle stop benefiting immediately.

Rage does not stack usefully. Two Rage spells on the same group do not double the boost; overlapping them extends the covered area or the covered time, which is a different and much narrower use.

When to drop it

The rule is engaged, not walking. A Rage over troops crossing open ground is spent on movement you did not need.

  • Drop it slightly ahead of the group so they move into the circle rather than out of it.
  • Drop it when the target matters. Raging a group killing a hut is money burnt; raging one hitting an Inferno Tower wins the attack.
  • Drop it when the group is large. Value scales with the number of troops covered, so a Rage over three survivors is not a rescue.

Common mistakes

  • Using it in the first fifteen seconds. Attacks are lost at the sixty-second mark, and that is where the spell should still be.
  • Raging troops that are about to die anyway. Rage adds damage, not survivability. If the problem is that your troops are dying, the answer is healing.
  • Doubling up out of panic. The second Rage is only worth it if the group needs a longer window, not a stronger one.

Frequently asked

Do Rage Spells stack?

Not in strength. Two Rage spells on the same troops do not double the boost. Overlapping them only helps if you need a bigger covered area or a longer covered window, such as when a push must travel a long way through a base.

When should I use a Rage Spell?

When your troops are already engaged with something worth killing quickly, and slightly ahead of them so they move into the circle. Rage dropped while an army is walking across open ground is largely wasted.
