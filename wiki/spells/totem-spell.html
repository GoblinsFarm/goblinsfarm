Totem Spell — Clash of Clans

Elixir

Updated: 26 August 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

The Totem Spell occupies one spell housing space with a five tile radius across four levels. Like the other single-slot spells it is built to solve a specific problem cheaply rather than to improve an entire attack.

What the numbers say

The single housing space means it competes with the dark spells for a slot rather than with Rage or Healing, so bringing one costs you very little of the loadout. The five tile radius means its effect lands on a group in one place, not on a push spread across a base.

As with every spell, the right question is which failure mode you are answering, as set out in spell mechanics: watch the replay of the attack that failed, and bring the spell that addresses what actually went wrong.

Frequently asked

How much housing space does the Totem Spell use?

One spell housing space, with a five tile radius. That makes it cheap to include alongside larger spells such as Rage or Healing rather than competing with them for room.
