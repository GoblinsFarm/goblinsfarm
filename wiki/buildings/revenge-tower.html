What the numbers say

50 damage per second against both air and ground, 11 tiles of range, 2 levels. For context, the Super Wizard Tower that unlocks alongside it lists 290.

A defence with a fifth of the damage and half again the reach is not competing on damage. Long range plus low damage almost always signals a building whose contribution is something other than raw output, and the name points the same way.

Placing it

Until the mechanic is pinned down, the safe reading is the one the range gives you: 11 tiles covers a lot of base, so it wants to be somewhere central where its circle overlaps the compartments attackers must enter. Do not displace a high-damage defence for it, and do not count it as your damage on that side.

What we have not verified

Behaviour not yet verified in game. The figures above are read from the game files, so they are solid. The special behaviour of this building is not something the files state plainly, and we would rather leave a gap here than invent a mechanic. If you play at this level and can describe what it actually does, tell us and you will be credited.

Frequently asked

What does the Revenge Tower do?

It unlocks at Town Hall 18 and covers both air and ground at 11 tiles for a listed 50 damage per second. That damage is far below other defences at the same Town Hall, which strongly suggests its value lies in a special behaviour rather than output, and we have not verified that behaviour in game yet.

Is the Revenge Tower a good defence?

Its raw damage is low, so it should not replace a heavy hitter in your core. Treat it as supplementary coverage until its actual mechanic is confirmed.
