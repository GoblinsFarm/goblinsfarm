Wall — Clash of Clans

Wall

Updated: 25 August 2026

Walls do not stop troops. They force ground units to take a longer route, which they spend under fire. They also consume unlimited resources instantly with no builder and no timer, which makes them the ideal place to put loot you cannot otherwise spend.

What they actually do

Ground troops path around walls unless breaking through is genuinely faster, so a wall converts distance into time spent under your defences. Air troops ignore them completely, and several ground units jump, tunnel or smash through.

The full mechanics are in walls and pathing.

Upgrading them

  • Never a priority, always a sink. Defences, heroes and research come first; walls take whatever is left.
  • No builder, no timer. You tap and it is done, which is exactly why surplus belongs here.
  • Loot in a wall cannot be stolen. Spending down to nothing before you log off is the single best defensive habit in the game.
  • Do not double-wall. Two adjacent segments cost twice as much and break to the same charge.

Frequently asked

Are walls worth upgrading in Clash of Clans?

Yes, but only with surplus. They need no builder and no timer and accept unlimited resources, which makes them the ideal place to dump loot before logging off, since resources spent on a wall cannot be stolen. They should never come before defences, heroes or research.

Do walls stop troops?

No. They force ground troops to take a longer path, which they spend under fire, and they do nothing at all against air troops, wall-jumpers or tunnelling units.
