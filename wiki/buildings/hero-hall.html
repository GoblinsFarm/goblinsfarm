Hero Hall — Clash of Clans

Army Building

Updated: 26 August 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

The Hero Hall unlocks at Town Hall 7, the same point the first hero arrives, and runs to 12 levels. It is the administrative centre for heroes and hero equipment, which makes it a gate on progression rather than a source of power in itself.

Why its level matters

Buildings that gate other upgrades are easy to deprioritise because they do nothing visible. That is a mistake here for the same reason it is a mistake with the Laboratory: if the Hero Hall is behind, the hero upgrades it governs are behind too, and hero levels are the largest single contributor to attacking power over time.

Treat it the way you treat storages. It is not exciting, and being short of it stops you spending on the things that are.

Equipment

Hero equipment is managed here, and the guidance on that is covered in hero equipment: concentrate ores on the few pieces you actually equip rather than spreading them across everything you own.

Frequently asked

What does the Hero Hall do?

It manages your heroes and their equipment, unlocking at Town Hall 7 alongside the first hero and running to 12 levels. It grants no direct combat power but gates hero progression, which does.

Should I prioritise the Hero Hall?

Yes, in the same unglamorous way you prioritise storages. It produces nothing itself, but leaving it behind holds back hero upgrades, and hero levels contribute more to attacking strength than almost anything else.
