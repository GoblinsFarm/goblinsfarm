Air Defense — Clash of Clans

Buildings

Defense

Updated: 16 September 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

The Air Defense deals heavy single-target damage to air units and cannot touch the ground at all. It is the most important building in a mid-game base, and the most commonly misplaced.

What it does

High damage to a single air target, no ground capability whatsoever. Dragons, Balloons, Lava Hounds and Minions are all built around either avoiding it or removing it, which tells you how central it is.

Placement is the whole game

More air attacks are beaten by placement than by levels.

  • Never two under one spell radius. If a single Rage or a stack of Balloons can reach two Air Defenses at once, you have one Air Defense.
  • Spread across sides. Coverage on three or four sides forces an attacker to deal with each one separately.
  • Place them deep. An Air Defense on the perimeter is removed by a handful of Lightning Spells or a few Balloons before the attack even starts.
  • Cover them with Archer Towers and air splash, so an attacker going for one takes fire from several buildings.

A base with three well-spread Air Defenses defeats air attacks that a base with three clustered ones loses to every time.

Frequently asked

Where should I place Air Defenses?

Deep inside the base and spread across different sides, never close enough together that one spell radius or one group of Balloons can reach two of them. Placement matters more than level: three spread Air Defenses beat three clustered ones decisively.

Can Air Defenses hit ground troops?

No, they target air units exclusively. That is why a base needs ground coverage from other defences even when its anti-air is strong.
