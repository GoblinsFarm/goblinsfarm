Archer Tower — Clash of Clans

Defense

Updated: 26 August 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

The Archer Tower is the first defence that covers both movement types. It deals single-target damage at good range to air and ground alike, which makes it the most generally useful building in an early base.

What it does

Attacks one target at a time at longer range than a Cannon, and crucially it can hit air units. In the early game it is often the only thing standing between your base and a small air attack.

Placement

  • Spread them. Because they cover both air and ground, clustering them wastes their flexibility.
  • Cover your Air Defences. An Archer Tower positioned so that an attacker going for an Air Defence is under fire from it makes air pushes much more expensive.
  • Do not treat them as anti-air on their own. Single-target damage does not stop a group of Dragons; it only slows one.

Frequently asked

Do Archer Towers hit air troops?

Yes, they target both air and ground, which makes them the most flexible defence available early. They are not a substitute for dedicated anti-air, since single-target damage does little against a group of air units.

Archer Tower or Cannon?

Archer Towers, generally, because they cover both movement types while Cannons cover only ground. Cannons are cheaper, so a base usually has both, with Archer Towers positioned where air coverage matters most.
