Cannon — Clash of Clans

Defense

Updated: 26 August 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

The Cannon is the simplest defence in the game: it shoots one ground unit at a time and cannot touch air at all. Cheap, reliable, and completely helpless against the air attacks that dominate the mid game.

What it does

Fires at one ground target at a time for solid damage. It cannot target air units under any circumstances, which is the fact that decides its placement and its limits.

Because it is single-target, it is good against a few large ground units and poor against a swarm of cheap ones, per defence mechanics.

Placement

  • Use them as compartment filler and outer defence, not as your core protection. They are cheap enough to spread widely.
  • Never rely on Cannons for coverage. A base whose ground defence is mostly Cannons falls to any swarm, and its air defence is nonexistent.
  • Place them so their circles overlap with splash defences, so a swarm entering their range is also under area damage.

Frequently asked

Can Cannons attack air troops?

No. Cannons target ground units only, which is why a base defended largely by Cannons is completely open to Dragon and Balloon attacks.

Are Cannons worth upgrading?

Yes, but never in preference to a missing category. A fourth Cannon adds far less than a first Air Defence, so close category gaps before adding depth to ground single-target coverage.
