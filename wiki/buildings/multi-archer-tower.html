Multi-Archer Tower — Clash of Clans

Defense

Updated: 26 August 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

The Multi-Archer Tower unlocks at Town Hall 16 and lists 60 damage per second across 10 tiles, hitting air and ground. That low figure is per target: the building's job is engaging several attackers simultaneously rather than deleting one.

What the numbers say

60 damage per second, 10 tiles, air and ground, 4 levels. Read alongside the Ricochet Cannon's 360, the number looks poor until you account for what it is doing with it, which is splitting attention across multiple attackers.

That makes it a swarm answer rather than a tank answer, the same role splash damage plays, arrived at differently. Against a single Golem it is close to irrelevant; against a wave of Minions or Barbarians it is working on all of them at once.

Placing it

Where swarms arrive, which usually means covering the outer compartments rather than the innermost one, since cheap massed troops rarely reach the core. Overlap it with splash damage so a swarm entering that area is taking area damage and multi-target fire together.

Frequently asked

Why is the Multi-Archer Tower's damage so low?

Because the figure is per target and the building engages several attackers at once. Its total output against a group is far higher than the listed number suggests, while against a single large unit it contributes very little.

What is the Multi-Archer Tower good against?

Swarms of cheap troops, air or ground, since it splits fire across several attackers simultaneously. It is a poor answer to a small number of high-hitpoint units.
