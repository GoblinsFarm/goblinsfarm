How to use this wiki

Every entry follows the same shape, so you can skim it in twenty seconds or read it in five minutes.

  • Quick facts at the top: role, housing space, targeting behaviour, what unlocks it.
  • Behaviour and mechanics: what the unit or building actually does, in words, including the quirks that are not in the in-game description.
  • How to use it and how to beat it: the two questions you actually came here with.
  • Stats by level: the number tables, at the bottom where they belong.

New to the game, or coming back after a long break? Start with the tutorials instead, then use the wiki as a reference while you play.

Where the numbers come from

Every stat table on this wiki is read directly from Clash of Clans' own game files, pulled from Supercell's asset CDN. Not transcribed by hand, not scraped from another wiki. The client build they came from is printed under every table, currently version 18.600.3.

That version matters, and we print it because a wiki that does not tell you how old its numbers are is asking you to trust them further than it has earned. We launched on an older source and found it was an entire Town Hall out of date; the methodology page tells that story, because stale data does not look stale, it just looks like data.

Where the files genuinely do not contain a value, the cell reads TODO rather than a guess. A dash means the field does not apply, which is a different thing. The written mechanics on every page hold regardless of balance changes; only the tables go stale, and now you know how stale.

How often is the wiki updated?

Stat tables are regenerated whenever we read a newer build of the game files, and the build number under every table tells you which one you are looking at. Mechanics pages are reviewed when an update changes how something works.
