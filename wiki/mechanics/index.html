Frequently asked

Do these mechanics change with updates?

The systems described here are stable across years; the numbers attached to them are not. Supercell has adjusted loot percentages, shield durations and league bonuses many times, but the underlying rules, that collectors hold a larger stealable share than storages, that damage grants a shield, that troops pick the nearest valid target, have held throughout.

Where should I start?

If you are farming, read loot mechanics, then shields and Village Guard, then matchmaking. If your attacks keep failing, read troop targeting and AI, then walls and pathing, then funnelling, in that order.
