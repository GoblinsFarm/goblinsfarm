What changes at this level

The Workshop unlocks siege machines, which carry your donated Clan Castle troops into the middle of a base rather than making them walk. This is a substantial change to how war attacks are built.

Upgrade order

The framework does not change between Town Halls, only the specifics. Keep the laboratory running, keep a hero upgrading where you have one, and keep every builder busy. Within that:

  • Heroes, continuously, still the main constraint.
  • Workshop and siege machine research.
  • Laboratory research on your war army.
  • Defence levels, prioritising the categories your replays show failing.
  • Walls with surplus.

The general reasoning behind this ordering is set out in upgrade priority.

Mistakes that cost you weeks here

Not requesting a siege machine. They are donatable, they cost your clanmates little, and attacking without one at this level is voluntarily giving up the value of your Clan Castle troops.

Frequently asked

What should I upgrade first at Town Hall 12?

Army camps and laboratory research before anything else, since they improve every attack you make at this level, then storages so you can afford later upgrades, then whichever defence category is weakest. Walls last, using surplus.

How long does Town Hall 12 take to complete?

It depends entirely on how consistently you keep builders, the laboratory and a hero running rather than on how much you play. Players who never leave a builder idle finish a Town Hall in a fraction of the time of players who log in twice a week, regardless of skill.
