What changes at this level

At the top of the tree the game is about optimisation rather than unlocks. The notable additions are the Firespitter, which has the longest range of any defence in the game at 16 tiles, and the Multi-Gear Tower, which covers air and ground at long range for heavy damage. Both are central defences rather than perimeter ones.

Upgrade order

The framework does not change between Town Halls, only the specifics. Keep the laboratory running, keep a hero upgrading where you have one, and keep every builder busy. Within that:

  • Heroes and equipment, continuously and forever.
  • Laboratory research, which at this level takes weeks per item.
  • Defence levels driven by replay evidence rather than by preference.
  • Walls with surplus, which at this point is a very large amount.

The general reasoning behind this ordering is set out in upgrade priority.

Mistakes that cost you weeks here

Playing without a plan. At this level the only lever left is not wasting timers, so the accounts that progress are the ones where a hero, the laboratory and every builder are running every day.

The other one is placing your new long-range defences forward. A 16 tile Firespitter already covers the perimeter from the middle of the base, so pushing it outward gains nothing and gets it killed early.

Frequently asked

What should I upgrade first at Town Hall 17?

Army camps and laboratory research before anything else, since they improve every attack you make at this level, then storages so you can afford later upgrades, then whichever defence category is weakest. Walls last, using surplus.

How long does Town Hall 17 take to complete?

It depends entirely on how consistently you keep builders, the laboratory and a hero running rather than on how much you play. Players who never leave a builder idle finish a Town Hall in a fraction of the time of players who log in twice a week, regardless of skill.
