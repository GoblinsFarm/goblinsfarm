Elixir Troops

BarbarianCheap, dumb, and still one of the best value troops in the game. ArcherShoots over walls, dies to a stiff breeze, and farms beautifully. GiantThe original tank. Slow, tough, and targets defences only. GoblinFast, greedy, and the best loot-per-elixir troop in the game when used correctly. Wall BreakerOne job, done once, at the exact moment you need it. BalloonFlies over everything, kills defences, dies to Air Defences. WizardRanged splash damage. Devastating behind a tank, useless in front of one. HealerNo damage, pure sustain, and completely helpless against anti-air. DragonThe most forgiving strong army in the mid game, and it is not close. P.E.K.K.AEnormous damage, enormous hitpoints, enormous housing cost. Baby DragonStronger when it flies alone, which is the opposite of how most players use it. MinerGoes under the walls instead of over them, and cannot be shot while travelling. Electro DragonChain lightning that punishes tightly packed bases. YetiA tank that spawns its own support as it takes damage. Dragon RiderAn air troop that goes for defences rather than whatever is nearest. Electro TitanA heavy ground unit that damages everything around it while it fights. Root RiderTargets defences and walks through walls, which is a rare combination. Thrower4 levels, 16 housing space Meteor Golem3 levels, 40 housing space

Dark Elixir Troops

MinionThe cheapest way to put damage in the air. Hog RiderJumps walls, targets defences, and ignores your entire base layout. ValkyrieSwings in a circle, which makes packed compartments her ideal home. GolemThe main tank of the dark elixir roster, and it splits when it dies. WitchSummons a continuous stream of skeletons, which is more valuable than her own damage. Lava HoundAn air tank that hunts Air Defences and splits when it dies. BowlerThrows a boulder that bounces, hitting the building behind the one it aimed at. Ice GolemA cheap tank whose death freezes the defences around it. HeadhunterA specialist that ignores buildings and hunts heroes. Apprentice WardenA small, deployable version of the Grand Warden's support aura. DruidSwitches between healing your troops and fighting alongside them. Furnace4 levels, 18 housing space Ruin Witch4 levels, 26 housing space
