What it does

Archers attack at range and, critically, that range lets them hit buildings on the other side of a wall without breaking it. Against a base whose collectors sit just inside the outer wall, a line of Archers takes them all without a single Wall Breaker.

The cost is fragility. Archers have very low hitpoints and die to almost anything that hits an area, so their survival depends entirely on not being where the damage is.

How to use it

Archers work behind something, or behind a wall.

  • Behind Barbarians or Giants. Let the tank engage first, drop Archers a moment later, and they shoot past it while it absorbs the fire.
  • Along a wall line. Spread them so they engage buildings inside without pathing around the base. This is the core of barch farming.
  • As clean-up. A few held back finish the exposed buildings your main push left standing.

The universal rule: never drop Archers into open ground in front of a live splash defence, and never stack them.

How to beat it

Any splash defence, and specifically splash coverage that reaches the outer ring where Archers stand. A base whose Mortars and Wizard Towers only cover the core will lose its perimeter collectors to Archers repeatedly. Pushing collectors inside a compartment, or making sure splash coverage extends outward, is the fix.

Frequently asked

Can Archers shoot over walls?

Yes. Their range allows them to attack buildings on the other side of a wall without breaking through, which is exactly why they are so effective at stripping collectors placed just inside the outer wall of a base.

Why do my Archers die so quickly?

Almost always because they were deployed in a stack or in open ground within range of a splash defence. Archers have very low hitpoints and need to be spread out and deployed behind something that absorbs damage.
