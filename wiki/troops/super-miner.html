Updated: 16 September 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

The Super Miner is a Home Village unit trained in the Barracks. It has 12 levels and takes up 24 housing space.

What the numbers say

Super Miner is a Super troop. It is trained in the Barracks and upgradeable through 12 levels. It takes 24 housing space, targets ground only and reaches 0.6 tiles.

Across those levels it gains hitpoints from 1,700 to 5,000 and damage from 80 to 265.
