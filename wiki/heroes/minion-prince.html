Minion Prince — Clash of Clans

Hero

Updated: 25 August 2026

The Minion Prince is a hero who flies, and that single property makes him behave unlike the ground heroes. Walls, compartments and pathing do not apply, so he can be sent directly at whatever you need dealt with.

What he does

He attacks from the air with hero-level durability and damage, and carries an ability like the other heroes. Because he flies, none of the geometry that constrains the Barbarian King or Archer Queen applies to him.

How to use him

  • Send him at things that are hard to reach on the ground, since the walls that would stop a ground hero are irrelevant.
  • Mind the anti-air. His weakness is the same as every air unit's, and a hero flown into concentrated Air Defence coverage dies as reliably as a Balloon.
  • Pair him with air armies, where his movement matches the rest of the push.

Upgrading him

Like every hero he is upgraded with dark elixir and unavailable while upgrading. Where he fits in your upgrade order depends on whether you attack primarily with air or ground, since a flying hero adds most to an air-based attack plan.

Frequently asked

What makes the Minion Prince different from other heroes?

He flies, so walls, compartments and ground pathing do not apply to him. That lets him be sent directly at targets a ground hero would have to walk around the base to reach, at the cost of being vulnerable to anti-air.

Is the Minion Prince worth upgrading?

It depends on how you attack. A flying hero adds most to an air-based attack plan, so if your armies are primarily ground-based, other heroes may return more per dark elixir spent.
