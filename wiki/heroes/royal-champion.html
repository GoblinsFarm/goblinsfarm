Royal Champion — Clash of Clans

Hero

Updated: 26 August 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

The Royal Champion attacks defences specifically and leaps over walls to reach them. That combination makes her the most self-directing hero: she goes to the buildings that matter without needing a funnel built for her.

What she does

She prefers defences, moves at range, and jumps walls rather than pathing around them. Her ability delivers a burst of damage to a target. Because she seeks defences, she can be sent into a base with a clear expectation of where she will go, which is unusual.

How to use her

  • Send her at a defensive cluster, since she will move between defences directly.
  • Support her. She is durable but not a tank, and a Royal Champion alone in the core dies to concentrated fire.
  • Time the ability on a high-value defence, not on whatever she happens to be hitting.

Upgrading her

Because she targets defences and ignores walls, her contribution is very consistent across different base layouts, which makes her levels reliably valuable rather than situationally so. Upgraded with dark elixir, unavailable while upgrading.

Frequently asked

Does the Royal Champion jump walls?

Yes. She leaps over walls to reach defences rather than pathing around them, which means base compartmentalisation does very little to slow her down and she needs much less funnelling than other heroes.

What does the Royal Champion target?

Defences specifically. She moves from one defence to the next and ignores other buildings until the defences are gone, which makes her unusually predictable and easy to plan around.
