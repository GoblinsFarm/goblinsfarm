Frequently asked

Which tutorial should I read first?

If you are new, getting started, then finding a good clan. If your attacks are failing, your first war attack followed by luring the Clan Castle. If you are short of resources, the farming loop. If your base keeps losing, reading your defence replays.

Are these tutorials up to date?

The routines described here are built on game mechanics that have been stable for years rather than on current balance numbers, so they do not go stale with each patch. Where a page cites specific figures it links to the wiki, where the data vintage is stated openly.
