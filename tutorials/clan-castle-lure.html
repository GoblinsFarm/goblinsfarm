How to Lure Clan Castle Troops

Updated: 26 August 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

The defending Clan Castle is the only thing on a base that comes after you. Pulling it out and killing it in open ground costs a fraction of what fighting it inside the base costs, and the routine takes under two minutes.

Cost
Two cheap troops and often one spell
Time
About 90 seconds
Skip it
Never, in war

Attacking into a live Clan Castle is the most common self-inflicted defeat in Clash of Clans. It is also entirely avoidable with a routine that becomes automatic after a dozen attacks.

Step by step

  1. Check whether the Castle is filled

    The scouting screen shows this. If it is empty, skip this whole routine and attack.

  2. Find the trigger radius

    Drop a single cheap troop near the edge of the base. If nothing happens, drop another slightly closer. One Barbarian at a time. The radius is larger than most players expect.

  3. Wait for the garrison to commit

    Once troops emerge, let them start chasing. Do not panic and deploy your army.

  4. Pull them into open ground

    Drop another cheap troop well away from the base, in a corner or an empty stretch. Defending troops chase across the whole map, so they will follow.

  5. Kill them there

    Away from the defences, surround them with a few cheap troops and drop a poison spell on the clump. Ramping damage plus bodies to hold them is the standard answer, and it is cheap.

  6. Now start your real attack

    The base is passive apart from heroes. Deploy the army you actually planned.

Common mistakes

  • Luring with the main army. The whole point is to spend almost nothing. Cheap troops only.
  • Luring next to the base. If the fight happens within range of the defences, you have not moved the problem, you have joined it.
  • Assuming the Castle is empty because nothing came out. You may not have reached the radius yet. Probe properly.
  • Skipping it to save time. The ninety seconds you save costs you the attack.

Frequently asked

How do I know if a base has Clan Castle troops?

The scouting screen shows whether the Clan Castle is filled. If it is, probe the trigger radius with single cheap troops before committing anything else, since the radius is usually larger than players expect.

What is the best way to kill lured Clan Castle troops?

Pull them well away from the base into open ground, surround them with a few cheap troops to hold them in place, and drop a Poison Spell on the clump. The ramping damage does the work for very little cost.
