Rushing Your Town Hall: What It Costs and How to Avoid It

Updated: 26 August 2026 · By the Goblins Farm team

Rushing is not forbidden and it is not always wrong. It is a trade, and most players make it without knowing what they are trading. This explains the exchange rate and what to do if you have already made it.

Applies to
Every Town Hall
Difficulty
Low
Recoverable
Yes, slowly

Rushing means upgrading the Town Hall while the base beneath it is far from complete. The Town Hall upgrade button being available is not a recommendation.

Step by step

  1. Understand what you gain

    Access to new buildings, troops and levels sooner. That is genuinely worth something, particularly if you find new content motivating.

  2. Understand what you pay

    Your defences are several tiers behind the players you now meet, so you lose more defences. Your army is behind the bases you are offered, so you win less. War matchmaking looks at what you own rather than whether it is upgraded, so your war matchups get harder. The result is a village that looks advanced and performs badly.

  3. Check whether you have already rushed

    Heroes far below the cap for your level, laboratory research several tiers behind, and defences that die instantly are the symptoms. If war feels impossible and farming feels unprofitable, that is usually why.

  4. If you have rushed, fix heroes first

    Hero levels are the largest contributor to attacking power and the slowest to recover, so they go first. Keep one upgrading permanently from now on.

  5. Then fix laboratory research

    Research improves every attack forever, and rushed accounts are usually badly behind here.

  6. Then close defence category gaps

    Not every building. The categories you are missing entirely, especially anti-air.

  7. Stop moving up until those three are reasonable

    The dig-out only works if you stop digging.

The moderate position

Most experienced players do not max everything and do not skip categories either. Get heroes, research, army camps and the key defences to a sensible level for the Town Hall, leave some decorative buildings behind, and move on. Leaving a few walls unupgraded is fine. Leaving your heroes ten levels behind is not.

Frequently asked

Is rushing bad in Clash of Clans?

It is a trade rather than a mistake. You get new content sooner and pay with weaker defences, a weaker army relative to opponents and harder war matchups. The problem is that most players make the trade without realising they are making it.

How do I fix a rushed base?

Heroes first, because they contribute most and recover slowest, then laboratory research, then whichever defence categories you are missing entirely. And stop upgrading the Town Hall until those three are reasonable for your level.
